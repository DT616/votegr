About

Privacy by design

How it works

We created an engine that runs locally in your browser that finds your voter information based on where you live, and generates directions to your polling place while avoiding license plate cameras. Everything the page needs is downloaded when you first visit, so your address never leaves your browser. The lookup and the directions are worked out right there. Once the page has loaded, it keeps working with no connection at all.

The files this page uses to compute your information were compiled from published city, state and county data sources. Additionally, we also retrieve and synchronize the data that DeFlock publishes on OpenStreetMap for projects like this to use.

Why make this?

  1. The Voter Information site requires you to submit your personal information and presumably logs lookups. You have to agree to a disclaimer to find this information, and every query is done on their servers. You should not have to hand over your details to find out your ward and precinct number, but that is a limitation of the state's tool. The City provides no lookup tool at all.
  2. Raise awareness that decentralized, free and open source software is possible, and we should foster more development and creativity to solve problems using principles that provide utility, maximize privacy and decentralization.
  3. Raise awareness about license plate cameras and how they might impact even the sacred act of voting. Nobody should have a reason to feel uneasy about being tracked to go vote. While being pulled over because a camera read your license plate is unlikely for most, it is a real possibility, as are false positives. If this site helps one eligible voter find the polls and vote, and results in the government having less data to collect, it was worth it.
